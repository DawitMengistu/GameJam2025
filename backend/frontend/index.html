    Rules

    How ATP works

    1. Each player secretly picks a 4-digit number.
    2. Digits are 1–9 only — no zeros, and each digit may appear once.
    3. On your turn, guess your opponent’s number.
    4. Feedback uses N (correct digit, wrong place) and O (correct digit in the correct place).
    5. First to guess the opponent’s full number wins. Missed turns / resign can end the match early.
    6. Use the digit tracker on the side to mark digits red / green / yellow while you think.
